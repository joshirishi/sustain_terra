## 3. Text

```text
1) Quick summary

• Refund percentages are based on timing (Table A/B).

• Card/UPI/netbanking refunds always return to the same payment instrument

• We initiate eligible refunds within 2 business days after we have complete information.

• Bank/network display times vary (most show within 5–7 working days ; cards can take up to 10 ).

• We do not deduct payment-gateway/MDR fees from the customer’s refund; any non-reversible processing fees are a merchant-side cost.

2) What we refund (by product type)

• Public/live cohort trainings (open enrolment)

Refunds follow Table A “No-show” and requests after a session begins are not eligible.

• Corporate/private trainings

Booking deposits and customisation fees are non-refundable once the engagement is confirmed. Prepaid balances beyond non-refundable items are refunded per your SoW and Table B .

• Consulting retainers / pre-paid hours

Non-refundable for completed work. Unused, pre-paid hours are refundable less time consumed, non-recoverable costs, and any SoW-specified cancellation charges.

• Self-paced / e-learning courses

7-day money-back guarantee if requested within 7 days of purchase and consumption is under 20% of the course (by lessons or time). If ≥ 20% consumed, or certificate/assessment unlocked, refunds do not apply.

• Digital downloads / templates / recordings

Non-refundable after delivery. If the file is corrupted or the content materially differs from the description, we will first replace it; if replacement isn’t feasible, we will refund.

• Bundles / package offers

If partially consumed, we pro-rate using the standard (non-discounted) price of items used and refund the balance after deducting any non-refundable items above.

Consumer rights: If any mandatory consumer right provides greater protection, that right prevails.

Consulting illustration:

Prepay ₹3,00,000 for 100 hours; you consume 22 hours. Refundable base = 78 hours × agreed hourly rate , less non-recoverable costs and any SoW cancellation fee.

4) Refund method, initiation, and timelines

• Destination: For card, UPI, and netbanking payments, refunds are always credited to the same payment instrument For direct bank transfers to STGV, we refund by NEFT to the same bank account you used.

• Initiation SLA: We approve and initiate eligible refunds within 2 business days after receiving complete information.

• Display times: Bank and network processing varies. Most refunds display within 5–7 working days ; cards can take up to 10 ; UPI may display sooner depending on the bank.

• Fees: There is no separate fee charged to you for a refund. Any payment-processing charges assessed to STGV at capture are generally not returned to STGV by the provider; this does not reduce your refund .

5) What’s included/excluded in the refund

• Included: Base fee and GST paid to STGV (credit note issued where required).

• Excluded: Irrecoverable third-party pass-throughs you authorised (e.g., booked venue/printing/travel for corporate sessions) and completed consulting work.

6) How to request a refund

• Email contactus@sustainterra.com with your name, order/invoice number, programme/engagement name, dates, and reason.

• If you paid by bank transfer, include account details used for payment (for verification).

• We acknowledge within 2 business days ; we may seek additional details/verification.

• We initiate eligible refunds within 2 business days of receiving complete information; your bank’s display time then applies.

7) Force majeure

If delivery is disrupted by events beyond reasonable control (e.g., natural disasters, government actions, widespread outages), we will first offer rescheduling or a credit note; if neither works, we will refund per Tables A/B.

8) Contact & updates

All refund requests and queries: contactus@sustainterra.com

Governing law: India.

We may update this policy; the latest version on our website applies.
```
