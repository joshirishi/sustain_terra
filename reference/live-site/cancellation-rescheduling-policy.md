## 3. Text

```text
1) Quick summary

• Public/live learners may cancel , reschedule once for free , or transfer a seat before cut-offs below.

• Corporate/private engagements follow SoW (Statement of Work) timelines; one free reschedule if requested early.

• Consulting sessions can be moved with short notice; completed work remains billable.

• If STGV changes a date or delivery mode, you may reschedule, take a credit, or get a full refund (see Refund Policy).

2) Definitions

• “Start date/time” = the scheduled start of the first live session of a programme or meeting.

• “Business day” = Monday–Friday, excluding Indian public holidays.

• “SoW” = signed Statement of Work for corporate/private/consulting engagements.

3) Public / live online cohorts (individual learners>

• A) Your options

• Cancel your enrolment → refund percentage is determined by timing (see Table A in the Refund Policy).

• Reschedule once for free to the next available cohort if you request ≥ 72 hours before the start.

• Additional moves: admin fee ₹1,000 per change.

• Requests < 72 hours before the start: reschedule fee = 25% of the amount paid .

• Seat transfer (substitution) → nominate another learner ≥ 24 hours before the start, free .

• No-show → counted as attended; no refund or free reschedule.

• B) How to act

Email contactus@sustainterra.com with your name, order/invoice ID, programme title/date, and your request (cancel | reschedule date | substitute’s name & email). We will confirm within 2 business days .

4) Corporate / private trainings

• A) Cancellation by client

• Follows the timelines below and any specific terms in your SoW (SoW prevails).

• Non-refundable items: booking deposit (typically 30%), customisation fees, and non-recoverable third-party costs (venue, travel).

• B) Rescheduling by client

• One free reschedule if requested ≥ 10 business days before the first session.

• Inside 10 business days , a reschedule charge of up to 25% of the programme fee may apply to cover trainer holds and confirmed logistics.

• New date should generally be within 60 days of the original, unless otherwise agreed in the SoW.

• C) Substitutions

• Update participant lists ≥ 1 business day before the session at no additional charge.

5) Consulting sessions / retainers

• Rescheduling interviews/workshops: give ≥ 2 business days notice to avoid chargeable holds.

• Cancellation: work completed to date and non-recoverable expenses remain billable per the SoW.

6) Changes initiated by STGV

We may postpone, reschedule, replace faculty, or shift delivery to live-online if required (e.g., trainer illness, low enrolment, force majeure). Your choices:

(a) move to a new date/batch, (b) receive a credit note valid 6 months , or (c) receive a full refund of fees paid (see Refund Policy). Our liability is limited to fees paid to STGV for the affected service.
```
