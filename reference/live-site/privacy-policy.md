## 3. Text

```text
Sustain Terra Green Ventures LLP (“Sustainterra”, “we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it when you use our website or enrol in our courses.

1) What we collect (minimum required)

We collect only the information needed to confirm your enrolment and deliver the course, such as:

• Name

• Email address

• Mobile number

• Organisation name (if applicable)

• Billing details for invoicing (only if required/provided)

Payments: If you pay via a payment gateway or bank transfer, we may receive payment confirmation details such as transaction reference/ID, amount, date/time, and payment status. We do not store your card number, CVV, or net-banking credentials on our systems.

2) How we use your data (limited purpose)

We use your data only to:

• Identify who has paid and confirm enrolment

• Send course access details and essential course communications

• Issue invoices/receipts and maintain lawful accounting/tax records

• Respond to support queries (for example, payment mismatch or access issues)

• Protect against misuse or fraud

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We do not use it for advertising profiling.

3) When we share data

We share limited data only with service providers who help us run the website and deliver services, such as:

• Payment gateways (to process/confirm payments)

• Email/communication providers (to send course information)

• Website hosting/security providers (to keep the site running and secure)

They are expected to use data only to provide services to us.

4) Cookies

We may use essential cookies for website functionality and security. If analytics are used, they are only to understand site performance and improve the website, not for targeted advertising. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

5) Data retention

We keep data only as long as needed for enrolment, course delivery, support, and legally required records (such as invoices and payment trails). After that, we delete or anonymise where feasible.

6) Your rights

Subject to applicable Indian law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may request access, correction, deletion (where lawful), or withdrawal of consent where applicable.

7) Contact

For privacy questions or requests, write to: contactus@sustainterra.com

Sustain Terra Green Ventures LLP

101/A, Spring Dale CHS, 3rd Golibar Rd., Santa Cruz (E), Mumbai – 400055, India

Phone: +91 9321472266

8) Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page with the revised “As of” date.
```
